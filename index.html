Studio Local

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it is

Studio Local is a personal desktop application that runs only on its owner's computer. It prepares videos for the owner's own YouTube channels (title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles and scheduled date) and, only after the owner reviews and clicks "Publish", uploads the video to the owner's channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only the owner. Studio Local is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not access any channel other than the ones the owner manages and explicitly authorizes.

How it uses Google data

  • It requests permission to upload videos and to set thumbnails, captions and scheduling on the owner's channels.
  • Every upload is started manually by the owner after reviewing the video. Nothing is published automatically.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared with anyone.
